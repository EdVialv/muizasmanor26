# MVP scope

## Product objective

Help residents, tourists, event organisers and prospective property buyers discover Latvian manor estates, understand what each location offers and contact the relevant manor or representative without searching across fragmented websites.

## Primary users

1. Visitors planning cultural trips.
2. Event organisers seeking a suitable venue.
3. Prospective buyers or investors looking to purchase a manor estate.
4. Real-estate agents representing manor sellers or prospective buyers.
5. Manor representatives maintaining listing information.
6. The platform administrator reviewing content and enquiries.

## Included in the MVP

- Responsive Latvian-first public website.
- Manor directory with pagination.
- Search by manor name and location.
- Filters for:
  - region;
  - public access;
  - accommodation;
  - food service;
  - event availability;
  - manor condition:
    - historical site with no surviving manor building;
    - damaged building, ranging from irreparable to repairable;
    - Class B — standing building requiring cosmetic repairs;
    - Class A — pristine or fully restored building;
  - current ownership:
    - private — private individual or legal entity;
    - public — municipality or the Latvian state;
  - historical owner family, noble house or dynasty.
- Map markers linked to manor pages.
- Manor detail pages with verified facts, images, location, services, contacts, condition, ownership classification, historical owner families and source/update metadata.
- Admin authentication and role-based access.
- Create, edit, publish and archive manor records.
- Event-enquiry form, consent record, spam protection and email notification.
- Basic SEO, accessibility, analytics, error monitoring and backups.

The MVP may display ownership and condition information for discovery purposes, but it is not a property transaction or brokerage platform. Sales listings, agent subscriptions and buyer-to-agent lead routing require separate commercial validation.

## Explicit non-goals before validation

- Property transactions, offers or legal due diligence.
- Paid real-estate listings or agent subscriptions.
- Online payments or deposits.
- Real-time availability and bookings.
- Paid subscriptions or premium placement.
- Multi-stop route optimisation.
- Restoration-service marketplace.
- Funding-application assistance.
- Native mobile applications.
- User reviews and public accounts.

## Validation target

The MVP is validated when, over a defined test period:

- users can find a relevant manor without assistance;
- prospective buyers and real-estate agents can identify potentially relevant estates by condition, ownership and historical association;
- manor representatives confirm that listing updates are manageable;
- genuine event enquiries are delivered reliably;
- analytics show repeat use or meaningful search-to-enquiry conversion;
- at least five manor operators, property representatives or agents agree to test a future commercial service.

## Public-launch minimum

- Published records are accurate and traceable to a source.
- Search, filters, map and detail pages work on mobile and desktop.
- Condition, ownership and historical-family data are clearly labelled and not presented as legal or property-sale advice.
- Admin access is protected and ordinary users cannot edit content.
- Enquiries reach the intended recipient and are logged.
- Privacy notice, cookie handling where required and consent text are present.
- Error monitoring, analytics and database backups are enabled.
- Accessibility and SEO smoke checks pass.
